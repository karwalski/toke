# Owner decisions and actions — 2026-11-12

This register lists every decision or action in the workspace that is waiting on the owner on
2026-11-12, with the options, the evidence, a recommendation, what it unblocks, and how urgent it
is. It replaces docs/owner-decisions-2026-10-01.md as the live list. That file stays as the record
of what was decided on 2026-10-01.

Sources: every open row in `docs/progress.md` at toke main `c26c1623` that is blocked on the owner,
awaits an owner review, or was decided but not carried out; the batch row files
`~/tk/tmpwork/Z1*_rows.json` and `Z1*_new.json` (Z100–Z146, several not yet merged or filed);
`~/tk/tmpwork/HANDOFF-2026-11-09.md`; and the 2026-11-09 run checkpoint. Before it was listed, each
item was checked against main or its branch to confirm it is still open. Numbers were re-measured
where that was cheap (marked **re-measured**); the rest are quoted from the row or batch report
named in the item.

**Nothing here is decided.** Each item carries a recommendation only.

**How to use this:** answer an item with its number and a letter (e.g. "1.2 a", or "1.2 rec" to
accept the recommendation). The main thread then records the decision in the row, lifts the block
and schedules the work.

**Urgency key:** **critical path**: it blocks the corpus → tokenizer → Gate 2 chain or the loke
release hand-off. **Quality**: a silent wrong result, a live wrong claim, or a security gap.
**Cosmetic**: a consistency or wording matter.

**Branch note (updated 2026-11-12, late):** batches Z131, Z133 and Z135–Z144 and Z146 are merged
into toke main (`integrate/z-wave-1110b`; conform 516/0) and their rows, with IDs 127.340–127.351,
